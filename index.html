Rank ladder
The 5 island rank laws
1
Past papers are the exam, not practice for it
Top rankers master every mark scheme. After each paper, spend equal time on the marking guide. Know the exact phrasing that scores.
2
Error log = your personal syllabus
Every wrong answer goes into Notion: topic, why you got it wrong, correct concept. Review it every Sunday. Your exam will target your weak spots.
3
Teaching younger students is a cheat code
The Protégé Effect: preparing to teach forces deeper encoding than studying alone. Mon and Wed teach sessions are secretly double study sessions.
4
Consistency destroys intensity
One 18-hour day then burnout is worse than 9 hours every single day. Your streak is more important than your daily hours. Never break the chain.
5
Final 2 weeks: zero new content
Only revision, error log review, and Anki. Sleep 8.5 hrs non-negotiably. Most candidates ruin themselves cramming new material right before the exam.
